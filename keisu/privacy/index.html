Keisu — Privacy Policy

Effective 3 September 2026

Keisu does not collect any data.

  • No personal data is collected, stored, or shared.
  • The app makes no network connections; nothing ever leaves your device.
  • All settings (session length, sounds, volumes, background image) are stored only on your device.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party software components.

Because the app collects nothing, there is no personal data to request, correct, delete, or opt out of. If a future version of Keisu ever changes any of the above — for example by offering optional paid content, with purchases processed by Apple — this page will be updated before that version ships.
