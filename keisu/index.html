Keisu

Keisu is a simple, calm gong timer for meditation on iPhone. Choose how long to sit, tap the bowl, and settle in: a hand-recorded gong opens the session, optional interval gongs mark the passage of time, and a closing gong ends it, while a soft ambient sound of your choice carries the whole sit.
