Siderale

Siderale tells you how good the coming nights will be for stargazing where you observe, and when: a score for each night with the factors behind it, the clear window, and what is up in the dark hours, judged for your instrument and your sky. The Alps at MeteoSwiss's 1–2 km resolution, anywhere else at the best resolution available; free, no account, no tracking.
